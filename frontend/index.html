Mission & Disaster Directive

National Disaster Coordination Architecture

In catastrophic humanitarian crises like floods, cyclones, and landslides, communication bottlenecks and fragmented registries account for over 64% of rescue delays. NEMO (National Emergency Management & Operations) provides the unified digital infrastructure that connects NDRF battalions, state disaster forces, medical shelters, and citizens in real-time.

NDMA

Our Origins & Mission

Initiated in response to severe monsoon flash floods and coastal cyclones across Odisha, Kerala, and Assam, NEMO was architected by emergency logistics officers, HAM radio operators, and geospatial engineers who recognized the urgent need for a unified digital coordination grid to replace siloed registers.

Today, NEMO provides high-resilience situational awareness for civil defense units, district disaster management authorities (DDMA), and voluntary relief brigades across India. Designed to function with zero-latency offline caching, the system replicates reliably even when local cellular networks suffer total blackout.

Technological Pillars

Core Disaster Response Framework

Engineered for sub-minute field deployment, automated deduplication, and low-bandwidth SATCOM operation.

Mesh & HAM Synchronization

Decentralized multi-node synchronization guarantees field workers can log data offline and automatically reconcile when satellite or HAM data uplinks reconnect.

Geospatial Sector Triage

Sub-meter GPS indexing with sector density heatmaps prevents search team overlap and focuses mobilized NDRF battalions on high-probability survival quadrants.

Intelligent Record Deduplication

Automated verification algorithms compare descriptions, clothing markers, and triage records to unify multi-source reports into a single actionable file.

Inter-Agency NDRF/SDRF Protocol

Full compliance with NDMA, CAP (Common Alerting Protocol), and EDXL emergency exchange standards for seamless collaboration with Indian Red Cross and district authorities.
